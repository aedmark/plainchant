THE WRITER

Writing software should help you write.

Margins, indentation, page breaks, file formats—Plainchant handles the machinery while you stay with the scene. It reads lightweight Fountain text and formats it live. There is nothing proprietary between you and your work, and nothing to configure before “FADE IN.”

Every page in Plainchant is both: the text you typed, and the page it becomes.

2.

02 The primer

INT. THE PRIMER - CONTINUOUS

Plainchant understands the shape of a script from the shape of your text. Use Tab to cycle an element when you want a hand; smart Enter takes it from there.

Four steps. That’s the whole primer.

01

INT. ARCADE - NIGHT

Set the scene. Start with INT. or EXT. and Plainchant recognises a scene heading.

02

MARA

Call your character. Put a name in capitals. Names you have used before are suggested as you type.

03

MARA (CONT'D)

(quietly)

One more life.

Let them speak. Press Enter after a character and dialogue begins. Parentheticals work naturally too.

04

Cut to: export

Take it with you. Print, save a PDF, or export for Fountain and Final Draft when the story is ready.

Note

Stuck? Tap the element bar—Scene, Action, Character, Dialogue—or press Tab. Plainchant never makes you memorise syntax.

03 The whole draft

INT. THE WHOLE DRAFT - LATER

Small enough to disappear. Capable enough to stay with a full-length script.

THE FLOW (typing)

Live formatting, smart Enter, autocomplete, focus mode and quick edits keep your hands on the story—not the toolbar.

THE ARCHIVE (the library)

Autosave, a script library, named versions and scene-by-scene comparisons give every draft a safe place to land.

THE SHAPE (the outline)

Outline, page view, scene and character stats, screen-time estimates, and video chapters reveal the shape of the work.
